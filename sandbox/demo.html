Loose Ends

Turns the people you keep meaning to see into plans. Each week it suggests friends you text but haven't seen, drafts the text, finds a time you're both free, and picks a spot nearby.

This is a demo. The people, plans and busy times are made up, and nothing here sends a text or touches a calendar. The real app runs on an iPhone and reads your iMessages and Google Calendar through a companion app on your Mac.
  • Swipe right to make the plan, left to skip
  • Tap the black plan to flip to another restaurant
  • Hold and drag it to change the time
  • Tap the text to edit it
